      Your data stays with you

      Course Files contacts only the Moodle website you explicitly connect to. Moodle may redirect its normal sign-in page to your school's identity provider.

      The ordinary Moodle token and course list are kept in Chrome's session storage, not browser sync, and are cleared on disconnect or browser restart. Any private token in Moodle's callback is discarded. We do not collect passwords, analytics, or file contents.

      The website address and request timing/cooldown metadata are remembered only on this device. Cooldowns survive disconnect and browser restart. File-list caches stay in this tab's memory. Folder access is granted through Chrome's folder chooser. No files are opened or executed automatically. Disconnect removes the connection and attempts to revoke its optional site permission; downloaded files are retained.

      This is unofficial and is not affiliated with Moodle or your institution. Use only where your school permits it. Pacing cannot guarantee access or prevent account/IP restrictions. School settings may prevent mobile/API access or route login to their own app. If Chrome asks to open another app, choose Cancel.
